## RINGKASAN EKSEKUTIF / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We conducted a formative usability evaluation without prior ethical determination. Participants were
not harmed: the study involved no intervention, no personal data, and a fully simulated stimulus. Our
own study protocol required ethics clearance before recruitment, and that requirement was not met. We
report it rather than relying on the absence of a discovery.

We are asking for a determination on the analysis and publication of already-deidentified data, not for
approval of the collection that has already happened.

**What we checked and did not find deficient.** The consent instrument's Part 3 recording-policy fields
— what is recorded, purpose, who may access, storage location, deletion date, and the contact for
deletion requests — were completed and approved before recording began, and were given to all ten
participants who consented to recording. Participant ages were verified against national ID cards and
confirmed as 19–21 for all ten, meeting the protocol's Gen Z criterion; no date of birth was
transcribed into the research data, as a privacy measure. Both points were previously flagged as
possible deficiencies on the basis of draft source files; on verification neither holds, and we record
the correction rather than the original suspicion.

## 1. PENYIMPANGAN YANG DI AKUI / DEPARTURES ACKNOWLEDGED

### 1.1 Pengumpulan data sebelum penentuan etik

**Fakta.** Sesi dijalankan tanpa pengajuan kepada Komite Etik Penelitian.

**Dasar.** §4.1 Panduan Klirens Etik BINUS menetapkan bahwa seluruh penelitian dalam lingkup §1.2
**harus diajukan sebelum rekrutmen atau pengumpulan data dimulai**, *"even when the researcher believes
the study carries only minimal risk."* Protokol penelitian kami sendiri, §9 butir (d), menjadikan
persetujuan protokol, sampel, instrumen, dan consent sebagai syarat mulai sesi formal.

**Mengapa terjadi.** Persetujuan peserta dianggap memadai sebagai persetujuan etik. Kelalaian ini
adalah kelalaian prosedural dari tim peneliti, dan seluruh tanggung jawab berada pada peneliti.

### 1.2 Instrumen persetujuan belum disahkan

**Fakta.** Naskah yang ditandatangani peserta adalah draf internal berstatus *"Belum disetujui"*, yang
meminta sendiri untuk diselaraskan dengan formulir resmi unit etik BINUS **sebelum diberikan kepada
peserta**. Paragraph tersebut tidak pernah diselaraskan.

**Dampak.** Peserta menerima informasi dan persetujuan yang substansinya memadai, namun dokumennya
tidak pernah melalui persetujuan institusi. Komite tidak pernah menilai apakah persetujuan yang
